### 1. The Historic Pipeline Track (Dunga Gali to Ayubia)
Constructed during the British colonial era to supply gravity-fed fresh mountain water to Murree, the Pipeline Track is a 4-kilometer flat trail that winds alongside the cliff edge beneath dense pine forest canopies. With zero steep gradients, it is the ideal gentle nature walk for families with children, photography enthusiasts, and senior citizens.

### 2. Mukshpuri Peak Trek (9,200 ft)
Mukshpuri is the second-highest summit in Galiyat. Starting from either Dunga Gali (shorter, steeper) or Nathia Gali near the Pines Hotel (gentler, shaded forest route), the trail winds through vibrant pine groves before opening onto a stunning grassy meadow plateau. On clear days, the summit delivers sweeping 360-degree panoramas of the Jhelum River, the snowline of Kashmir, and the distant Nanga Parbat massif.

### 3. Miranjani Peak Expedition (9,776 ft)
For experienced hikers seeking a rewarding physical workout, Miranjani Peak is the highest point in the Abbottabad district. Starting near the Governor House in Nathia Gali, this 4.9 km climb ascends through thick oak and pine forests onto an open ridge. Hikers are rewarded with breathtaking vistas of Tarbela Lake, Abbottabad valley, and the snow-draped peaks of Swat and Kaghan Valley.

## 4. Packing Essentials for High-Altitude Galiyat Treks
When packing for Nathia Gali and the Galiyat highlands:
1. **Layered Thermal Apparel:** Bring a windbreaker/fleece jacket even during peak summer months, as temperatures drop rapidly after sunset.
2. **Sturdy Trekking Footwear:** Wear trail running sneakers or hiking boots with deep rubber lugs for wet pine needle trails.
3. **Rain Protection:** Mountain weather changes quickly; always carry a compact waterproof poncho or travel umbrella.
4. **Hydration & Snacks:** Carry a reusable water flask, high-energy nuts, and dried fruit to sustain energy on uphill climbs.

## 5. Eco-Tourism & Leave-No-Trace Principles
The fragile alpine ecosystem of Ayubia and Nathia Gali National Parks requires responsible stewardship from every visitor:
* **Never Feed Wildlife:** Human food alters the foraging behavior and digestive health of wild Rhesus Macaques and bird species.
* **Carry All Waste Out:** Never leave single-use plastics or wrappers along trekking paths; carry them back to disposal bins in the town center.
* **Stay on Designated Trails:** Avoid cutting across switchbacks to prevent delicate meadow topsoil erosion.
