## 5. Winter Road Safety & Vehicle Preparation Checklist
Driving in snowy mountain terrain requires preparation. Ensure you follow standard guidelines:
* **Tire Health & Snow Chains:** Verify that tires have deep tread. Carry portable snow chains when traversing upper ridges after heavy snowfall.
* **Anti-Freeze & Wiper Fluid:** Ensure your radiator contains high-grade anti-freeze coolant and windshield wiper fluid is formulated for sub-zero temperatures.
* **Battery & Brakes Inspection:** Cold weather reduces battery cranking power; have your vehicle battery and brake pads tested prior to departure.
* **Check Live Weather Updates:** Monitor official Pakistan Meteorological Department (PMD) bulletins before heading into high-altitude corridors.
