Frequently Asked Questions About Furnished Apartments in Nathia Gali

How far are your furnished apartments in Nathia Gali from Ayubia National Park and the Pipeline Track?

Our furnished apartments and chalets are situated directly along the pine ridge, located just 5 to 10 minutes from the famous Pipeline Walking Track, Ayubia chairlift, Governor House, and Nathia Gali Club.

Do your furnished apartments in Nathia Gali have heating and hot water?

Yes! All Kaghan Stay furnished chalets and apartments in Nathia Gali are equipped with inverter heating systems, heavy thermal quilts, and 24/7 continuous hot water geysers.

Can guests cook their own meals in the furnished apartments?

Yes, each furnished apartment includes a fully equipped kitchen with stove, microwave, refrigerator, cooking pots, and dining tableware for self-catering.
