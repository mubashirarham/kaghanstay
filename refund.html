Transparent Guarantee

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 2026  |  Last Updated: August 2026  |  Helpline Desk

1 48-Hour Full Refund Policy

At KPH Stay, we are committed to transparent booking terms. We offer a 100% Full Refund on all online room reservations made via our website (https://kphstay.com) if the cancellation is submitted within 48 hours of original booking confirmation, provided that check-in is at least 72 hours away.

2 Refund Processing via PayFast (5-7 Business Days)

All refunds are executed through our authorized payment gateway provider, PayFast:

  • Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original funding source (debit card, credit card, bank account, JazzCash, or EasyPaisa).
  • Refunds typically post to your bank account or card balance within 5 to 7 business days.
  • KPH Stay does not deduct arbitrary cancellation or administrative fees on eligible refunds.

3 How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To submit an official refund request:

Email: Send a message to info@kphstay.com with subject "Refund Request - [Your Booking ID]".

Helpline: Call our reservations desk at +92 334 009 1127 or +92 51 8461975.

Track Portal: Enter your reference on the Track Stay page to request reschedule or cancellation.

4 Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds are not granted under the following circumstances:

  • Cancellations requested after the 48-hour free cancellation window has elapsed.
  • Cancellations requested less than 72 hours prior to scheduled check-in time.
  • Guest "No-Shows" (failure to arrive on the confirmed check-in date without prior approved cancellation).
  • Early check-outs after taking physical possession of the suite.

5 Duplicate Payment Resolution

In the event of an internet disruption resulting in a duplicate debit by PayFast for the identical reservation, notify us at info@kphstay.com. Upon instantaneous verification in our payment dashboard, the duplicate transaction will be 100% refunded within 5 to 7 business days.
