Pricing & Payment Policy

Last Updated: August 2026  |  Questions? Contact Lobby

1. Base Currency & Pricing Transparency

All room rates, suite prices, packages, extra guest charges, and add-on service fees listed on KPH Stay (https://www.kphstay.com) are quoted and billed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR / Rs.).

We maintain strict upfront pricing transparency. The total amount displayed at the final checkout screen prior to payment authorization includes all applicable room charges, service fees, and taxes. There are no hidden fees or unquoted surcharges.

2. Accepted Payment Methods (PayFast Integration)

Online payment transactions on KPH Stay are securely processed through PayFast (APPS - Payment Systems Provider / PSO licensed by the State Bank of Pakistan). We accept the following payment options:

  • Visa & Mastercard Debit and Credit Cards (Local & International)
  • PayPak & UnionPay Cards
  • Direct Bank Accounts & Mobile Wallets (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, NayaPay, SadaPay, and 1LINK enabled bank accounts)

3. Applicable Taxes & Fees

Unless explicitly stated otherwise during the booking process, room prices are subject to provincial sales tax (PST / GST) and federal levies where applicable by the laws of Pakistan. All tax amounts are calculated dynamically and displayed clearly before you submit payment.

4. Dynamic Pricing & Rate Adjustments

Room rates in Islamabad and Nathia Gali vary according to season, weekend demand, national holidays, and occupancy levels. Once a booking is confirmed and paid for, the nightly rate for that specific reservation is locked and guaranteed against future price increases.

5. Digital Invoice & Instant Confirmation

Upon successful completion of a transaction via PayFast or reservation with Pay on Check-in, a digital booking voucher and payment confirmation receipt bearing your unique Booking ID (BK-XXXX) is instantly issued and dispatched to your registered email address. You may also view and download invoices anytime from your guest dashboard.

6. International Cardholders & Currency Conversion

If you pay using an international credit card issued outside Pakistan, your bank or card issuer will convert the PKR transaction amount to your home currency (e.g. USD, GBP, EUR) based on their prevailing exchange rates and foreign transaction policies.
